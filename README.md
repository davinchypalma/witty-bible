# Witty Bible for iPhone

The web app of Witty Bible: find the Bible verse you are looking for, by theme, by situation or by the words you
remember. The AI search runs on the phone itself, and once the app is on the Home Screen it works with no internet.

**Open it on the iPhone, in Safari:** https://davinchypalma.github.io/witty-bible/
then tap the Share button and **Add to Home Screen**.

In English, French and Spanish: the app speaks the language of the Bible chosen.

These are the app's built files, served as they are by GitHub Pages. Bible texts: KJV, BSB, UKJV, NHEB, Darby,
Louis Segond 1910, Darby (French), Martin, Crampon, Perret-Gentil, Reina-Valera 1909 and Reina-Valera 1865 (Spanish),
all public domain; Strong's dictionaries
(public domain, JSON edition by Open Scriptures, CC BY-SA); themes and cross references from OpenBible.info (CC BY).
The NIV and NKJV are not here: the app shows them only with the user's own free YouVersion key.
